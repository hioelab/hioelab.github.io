Seungbeom Kim 김승범

Associate Professor, College of Business Administration, Hongik University (since 2015)홍익대학교 경영대학 부교수 (2015년~)

Seungbeom Kim is an Associate Professor in the College of Business Administration at Hongik University. His research bridges behavioral science and data science to study operations management, revenue management, quality management, and customer experience. He received his Ph.D. in Operations Management from the Marshall School of Business at the University of Southern California, with a dissertation on bounded rationality and revenue management.

He has led applied projects with firms and public institutions including Apple Korea, LG Uplus, Nu Skin Korea, and several government ministries and research foundations, and is Co-Founder & COO of Unimindlab, a disability-inclusive technology company. He served as Department Chair (2024–2025) and Deputy Director of the Convergence and Open Sharing System for Biohealth at Hongik University.

김승범 교수는 홍익대학교 경영대학 부교수로, 행동과학과 데이터과학을 기반으로 운영 관리, 수익 관리, 품질 경영, 고객 경험을 연구합니다. University of Southern California, Marshall School of Business에서 운영관리 전공으로 박사 학위를 취득했으며, 박사 논문 주제는 제한된 합리성과 수익 관리였습니다.

Apple Korea, LG Uplus, 뉴스킨코리아 등 기업과 정부 부처·연구재단의 응용 프로젝트를 다수 수행했으며, 장애 포용 기술 기업 유니마인드랩의 공동창업자 겸 COO입니다. 홍익대 경영대학 학과장(2024–2025), 바이오헬스 융합·개방공유체계 부단장을 역임했습니다.
